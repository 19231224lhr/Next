\subsection{Continuous Spending Without Per-Hop Settlement}
\label{sec:eval-continuation}

Three NoSync wallets rotate 100~CAL through a 100-hop chain supported by
nine services. Each hop uses a distinct prefunded final FUEL output;
neither fee change nor refund from the preceding payment delays the next
payment. We compare three clients. F continues after certified receipt.
W also waits for the wallet to verify public execution. M adds a member
gate after W's wait: it polls the four members every 5\,ms and proceeds
once any three report the exact parent fact as observed. The gate does
not wait for the fourth member or permit a timed-out request to proceed.
Genesis fee inputs are loaded before the run. Each mode runs three times
in rotated F/W/M, W/M/F, and M/F/W order after a three-second warm-up.
All modes record actual requests and attempts.

The endpoint is $\mathsf{Sent}_1\rightarrow\mathsf{Ready}_{100}$:
first actual HTTP send to the hundredth verified, atomically recorded
receipt. It includes subsequent construction and retries, and W/M's 99
intermediate waits, but excludes the last payment's public confirmation.
All modes start with a final principal input. Thereafter F carries the
immediate parent's TXCer as a certificate input; W/M use final inputs.
Thus the comparison measures complete client modes, including their
different evidence paths.

Median chain times are 4.599\,s (F), 64.216\,s (W), and 64.307\,s (M),
with respective run ranges 4.595--4.695, 64.137--64.394, and
64.284--64.339\,s (Fig.~\ref{fig:continuation}). Each F and M run uses
exactly 100 HTTP attempts. W uses 123, 112, and 125: the 60 failed attempts
and their retry sleeps together account for 1.124--2.434\,s per run.
Removing these retries therefore leaves the main timing gap intact.
The 99 public waits total 57.981--58.323\,s in W and
58.981--59.315\,s in M; M's additional gates total 1.544--1.694\,s.
These gates measure client-visible readiness, including polling.
The median M/F ratio is 13.98 when computed from the two mode medians;
it is a continuation-mode result, not a consensus speedup.

All 900 payments close and pass accounting, input, fee, replica, and
outbox checks. The three F runs create 2, 3, and 5 actual
child-before-source obligations, all fulfilled by their sources;
none requires compensation. These chains measure certified continuation,
while the mixed-load experiment below exercises compensation and repayment.
Supplement~S4 reports every run, disjoint client-stage totals, and
application-commit and wallet-following checkpoints.
