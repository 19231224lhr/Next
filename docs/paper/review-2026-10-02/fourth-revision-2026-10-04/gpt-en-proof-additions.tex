The new \texttt{TestPublicAuthorizationAtABCIEntry} submits encoded
bytes whose authorization fields were subsequently tampered with
directly to the three ABCI interfaces, checking rejection, empty
\texttt{pendingChanges}, and unchanged business state.
Sender-side encoding failures are recorded separately from
receiver-side rejection. Receiver-side decoding, binding checks,

attributed to the guard actually reached, and a codec rejection
