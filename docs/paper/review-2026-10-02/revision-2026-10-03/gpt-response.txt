  - heading "英文实验段落（约130词）" [level=2]
  - paragraph: "We compared authorized historical representation with an append-only decision-index design under the same executed prefix and economic query contract. Three independent processes issued 21,600 interleaved queries over real bbolt and BlockStore state, with warm storage pages but no decoded-block or result cache. Both paths returned identical funding source, original input identity, transaction and coordinates, amount, compensation decision, and current obligation state across compensated, represented, materialized, and repaid cases. For compensated-slot point queries, Canonical representation reduced median latency by 7.44–12.32%; whole-block reductions were 1.49–3.88%, with most of the point-query difference arising from block layout and decoding rather than decision resolution. This benefit carries explicit cost: representation adds revision, task, and BlockStore records and required 43–214 ms of one-time local representation work across the tested configurations. We therefore treat representation as a read-layout and historical-interface tradeoff, not an economic necessity."
