\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Next lets a recipient keep spending on certified evidence while the issuing organization answers, from a funded reserve, for a source that has not executed. For four-member organizations and a four-validator committee with at most one static Byzantine member each, we prove unique consumption, funded outstanding CAL liability, and, for source-closed, conflict-free payment sets, delay-neutral terminal CAL holdings. A certificate does not guarantee public execution, since one global intent admits one payment. On one host, a 100-hop chain reaches its last certified receipt in 4.557\,s against 64.476\,s with per-hop waiting, and compensation and repayment complete with adaptation paused. Authorized representation exposes the reserve debit at the original coordinates for honest replicas that executed the prefix, at the cost of added records and maintenance. These results do not cover general committee sizes, untrusted remote readers, or incentives.
