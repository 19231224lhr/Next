\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Next separates three paths that continuous payments otherwise serialize. One-round certification lets a recipient spend a newly certified output, so on one host with synchronous member commits a 100-hop chain reaches its last certified receipt in 4.557\,s rather than 64.476\,s. Direct responsibility removes the wait for the source: reserve-backed signing capacity covers the outstanding CAL liability of every certificate, original signers can resubmit withheld sources from published certificates, and a public decision can close a due Open obligation under the stated protocol conditions, which a late source repays. In the mixed workload, compensation and repayment completed with all adaptation services paused. The optional authorized representation records the reserve debit at the original coordinates while preserving successor references and the complete BlockID, at a measured storage and maintenance cost. Continuation, economic closure, and historical record each proceed on their own evidence.
