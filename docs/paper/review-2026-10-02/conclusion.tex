\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Next supports continuous UTXO payments by separating certified receipt
from public execution and source resolution. Its layered architecture
lets recipients request successors while evidence replication proceeds
in the background. Direct issuer obligations permit missing-source
execution with real reserve backing; cumulative accounting covers
hidden certificates, and late-source recovery repays the issuing reserve.
For completed source sets, delay preserves the final allocation of principal. Authorized repair
then preserves fixed payment fields and complete block identities while
batching representation work and allowing installation to advance
through committed revisions. Original-history replay remains independent
of installation timing. The safety analysis connects these mechanisms
to unique consumption, funded liabilities, and asset conservation.
Dependent-payment measurements and disk-backed repair executions
demonstrate the implemented paths. Within the registered-organization
model, Next makes recipient continuation the foreground operation and
source resolution a funded, auditable background responsibility.
