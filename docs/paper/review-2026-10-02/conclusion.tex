\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Next lets a recipient keep spending on certified evidence while an
identified, funded issuer answers for each missing source. On the baseline
build, a 100-hop chain reaches its last certified receipt in a median
158.846\,ms, where waiting for public confirmation between hops takes
65.204\,s, and a nine-run matrix closes 21,600 payments and repays all 216
compensations. Two further properties govern how a missing source is closed. First, delay
changes only the temporary exposure of reserves: when all sources execute,
every order of arrivals and compensations ends with the same final
holders. Second, the compensation decision is a public record that needs
only the committee. The representation path that later records the debit in
the stored block can lag or stop without holding the obligation open. The
decision-first runs confirm this, since compensation and repayment
complete with all four adaptation services paused, and a withheld source
executes through resubmission by the original signers. Together, these paths separate payment continuation from source delivery
and economic closure from historical materialization.
