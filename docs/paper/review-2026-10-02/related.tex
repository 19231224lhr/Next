\section{Background and Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

Next connects certificate-based continuation, reserve-backed public
execution, and authorized historical repair. The closest work establishes
precedents for certification, collateral, and controlled editing. We
position Next by the responsibility assigned to a missing source and
by the payment and block identities preserved when that responsibility
is resolved.

\subsection{Certificate-Based Payments and Execution}

FastPay uses Byzantine consistent broadcast for low-latency payments
and supports a primary-ledger deployment backed by prepaid
funds~\cite{fastpay}. Its redeemability argument permits confirmation of
a valid certificate before all corresponding incoming transfers are
locally confirmed, provided the sender's missing earlier outgoing
certificates are supplied. Redemption provides a certificate-based route
to the primary funding pool. Zef combines disposable accounts with
private, transferable coins. Normal spending uses current coin
certificates and consumption evidence rather than the complete ancestor
transaction history~\cite{zef}.

Sui Lutris combines broadcast execution for owned objects with consensus
for shared-object interactions~\cite{lutris}. Stingray supports concurrent
transactions through Byzantine bounded counters and
FastUnlock~\cite{stingray}; overlapping capacities and reclamation are
close precedents for Next's resource accounting. Its commutative
workloads address contention differently from a payment chain in which
each transaction consumes its predecessor's output. Next focuses on
publicly executing a certified UTXO consumption before the source
transaction executes, while registering a funded obligation against the direct
issuer. Full-output reservation and cumulative loss retention account
for both publicly registered liabilities and commitments represented by
hidden certificates.

\subsection{Channels and Collateralized Guarantees}

Blitz uses pay-or-revoke to replace two-phase commitment in channel
payments, while Donner develops virtual channels that address the Domino
attack~\cite{blitz,donner}. CryptoMaze provides atomic multipath
channel payments with unlinkable partial payments while avoiding duplicate
off-chain contracts on shared edges~\cite{cryptomaze}. These mechanisms
operate through channel arrangements. Next uses a native ledger with
registered consumption routes and organization reserves, so counterparties
need neither bilateral channels nor a prefunded channel route between them.

Snappy protects merchants accepting fast on-chain payments through
collateralized approvals. Reusable payment guarantees combine privacy
with customer collateral reusable across defined time
windows~\cite{snappy,reusable}. Next instead separates the user's payment
principal from additional organizational backing. Its compensation
closes the missing source of an already executed child without issuing
another principal credit to that child's recipient.

HyperService uses a public Network Status Blockchain and an Insurance
Smart Contract to adjudicate cross-chain execution violations and
financially reverse the effects of committed transactions~\cite{web3}. Next addresses
a different responsibility boundary within one native UTXO ledger:
compensation settles a direct issuer obligation while preserving the
principal effects of already executed successors.

\subsection{Controlled Editing and Historical Consistency}

Chameleon hashes permit authorized alternative messages under the same
digest and underpin editable-blockchain designs~\cite{redactable}.
SDR studies decentralized attribute-based edit authority and dynamic
delegation; Escaping moves committee selection and voting outside the
consensus layer to reduce redaction waiting~\cite{sdr,escaping}.
Reconstructing Chameleon Hash develops stronger definitions of
indistinguishability and collision resistance~\cite{reconstructCH}.
Next uses established adaptation primitives, with public payment-state
checks supplying the authority to apply a compatible replacement.

Reparo provides a publicly verifiable repair layer and can re-execute
affected transactions when repairing state~\cite{reparo}. Next fixes
payment authorizations and outputs: a public repair command accounts
for compensation, and historical installation replaces the permitted
funding representation without re-executing successors. Original commands
and subsequent repairs remain the economic replay history.

RedactChain coordinates concurrent same-block editing conflicts and
preprocessing~\cite{redactchain}. CDEdit uses reusable privilege tokens
to reduce repeated token and key generation and verification across
multiple edits~\cite{cdedit}. Next's batch retains each obligation's
eligibility, reserve debit, and terminal-state check while sharing the
final block representation. Its complete block identity combines the
stable header hash with the original part-set header. Publicly committed
logical revisions authorize exact replacement bodies; physical
installation can advance directly to a later authorized revision.
This separates once-only economic compensation from the physical
installation of representation updates while preserving successor references and replay of the same
public command history.
