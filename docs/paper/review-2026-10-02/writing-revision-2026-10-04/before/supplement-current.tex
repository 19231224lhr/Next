\noindent\textbf{Subsequent reclamation regression.}
Build \texttt{bf71c4d} adds the superseded-partial-approval transition. Directed tests cover exact ordinary and fee-input conflicts, duplicate processing, restart, refusal to re-sign, and the disjoint-input Intent counterexample. A separate fresh-start nine-service run with synchronous member storage completes 1,000 user-funded payments offered at 100 payments/s (9.990-s send span, 10.879-s total), and a 100-hop fast chain reaches its last certified receipt in 4.422 s and closes in 5.014 s. Both runs have matching committee state and empty outboxes. These correctness regressions do not replace the performance measurements of \texttt{721800c}.
