需求与用途（约 205 词）

latex
\noindent\textbf{Use: block-record archival review.}
Consider a review that runs on an executed local replica and keeps one record per historical block. Before compensation, it saves the commit evidence and the coordinates $(h,j,k)$ of a consuming payment. After the decision and its representation commit, it exports the authorized body with the exact Revision and Task, rebuilds the complete BlockID through Next's block, part, and signature verification interfaces, using each version's revision and openings, and checks the body against the saved commit, whose votes sign the block hash together with the part-set header. It then reads the reserve-debit reference from the designated input field and joins it with the permanent decision and the obligation state at $H$. Because the funding slot lies outside the fields covered by consensus transaction hashes, the header hash alone does not bind it; the complete identity does. The original block with the decision index, or a materialized funding view, supports the same economic review by keeping the original body and an external annotation. C3 instead provides a replaceable body representation of that block. It confers no stronger authorization, is not needed for economic closure, and is not verifiable by unmodified CometBFT; it is an optional record format whose workflow our prototype exercises.

C3 贡献（约 80 词）

latex
\noindent\textbf{C3: Optional authorized block-body representation.}
After a compensation decision commits, restricted chameleon-hash commands can replace the funding reference of the compensated input with its reserve-debit reference while preserving owner authorizations, successor references, and the complete BlockID. A local replica can then export, at the original coordinates, a block body that names the actual funding and verifies against the original commit, together with the Revision, Task, decision, and obligation state that authorize and interpret it. Economic closure does not depend on this layer.

“prototype exercises”是有意的措辞：它表示原型走通了这个工作流程，既不暗示存在用户需求调查，也不暗示有真实部署。
