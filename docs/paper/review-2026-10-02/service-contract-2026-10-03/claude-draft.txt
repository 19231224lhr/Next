\noindent\textbf{Admission.}
Any client may act as collector; this grants neither spending authority nor organization-funded fees. Each member decides each request locally: in one transaction it checks its Intent record, every input lock, the fee, the grant, and its Worker slice. If the slice cannot cover $a_T$, the member rejects the request, commits nothing, and returns no signature. It signs only after a successful commit, and an idempotent retry reuses the stored signature without new capacity. A wallet records a certified receipt only after verifying three matching approvals, the organization configuration, the network, its own ownership, and the output commitment. One member's refusal is not a global admission decision, and the absence of a QC does not imply the absence of reservations at members that approved.

\noindent\textbf{Unenforced conditions.}
The protocol enforces neither Subject-level admission for CAL guarantees nor a bound on unfinished approvals across collectors; gateway concurrency limits and fee-sponsorship policies provide neither. The capacity condition of Section~\ref{sec:security-composition} therefore holds only under a controlled workload or an external deployment restriction, which this paper assumes rather than implements.


\noindent\textbf{Residual risk.}
The direct issuer bears the CAL of a consumed output whose source never executes, including a source rejected publicly by an Intent conflict that its members could not observe. Section~\ref{sec:security-composition} bounds this exposure; FUEL fees do not insure it.

\noindent\textbf{Availability and guarantor risk.}
Once a consumer exposes the source QC, at least two honest source signers retain its submission material (Lemma~\ref{lem:security-resubmission}), but resubmission cannot overcome a permanent Intent conflict; a due obligation can instead close without adaptation (Lemma~\ref{lem:security-closure}). At every prefix,
\[
  0\le S_g=\sum_{c\in C_g}\bigl(p_c-\mathsf{Rec}_c\bigr)\le Q_g\le B_g .
\]
For a fixed grant without further funding, this bounds net unrepaid compensation, not lifetime gross Paid. An unexecuted source keeps $r_{m,c}=a_c$ at each honest signer, so certification from a slice can stop while reserve principal remains. A top-up raises member shares without clearing these reservations, and the added authorization is exposed in the same way. Permanently unexecuted sources fall outside Theorem~\ref{thm:security-neutrality}. Without the restriction of Section~\ref{sec:model-contract}, an issuer therefore accepts adversarially induced reserve loss and retained capacity, not only accidental source failures; Supplement~S3 records one such trajectory.

\noindent\textbf{Intent-conflict risk ledger.}
With $B_A=300$, each member holds $\lfloor 2B_A/3\rfloor=200$ in one Worker, and each A-side source reserves $a=100$. In each of two cross-organization rounds, a QC-bearing A-side source and a B-side payment share one Intent and spend disjoint inputs; the B side executes first. Table~\ref{tab:s3-ledger} lists the state after each round's compensation.

Public Reserved returning to zero does not restore source-signer capacity, and the fourth member alone cannot complete a quorum. The rejected sources' inputs stay publicly unspent and locally locked. Each later A-side request spends the preceding descendant and needs a fresh Intent, a usable route, and fees; the final descendant is spent through B. In the user-funded trace, both rejected sources keep their complete 10{,}000-FUEL inputs locked, not only their 1{,}000-FUEL escrow maxima. The two rounds and the final descendant spend 480 FUEL (430 rewards, 50 burned) from 5{,}000 of escrow maxima, and 4{,}520 is refunded. In the organization-funded trace, B's authorized fee account pays the 480 FUEL, A's public FUEL is unchanged, A's local fee reservations remain, and the CAL loss stays with A. CAL and FUEL are reported separately.

For the same three source signers, one fixed Worker, no other reservations, no release, and reservation $a$ per round, those signers can approve $\lfloor\lfloor 2B/3\rfloor/a\rfloor$ rounds, here two, ending with $S_g=Q_g=200\le B_A$. This describes the trajectory, not a maximum over other signer sets or schedules. A top-up adds the share difference without clearing retained reservations.

服务契约。 认证到账只说明收款人持有一个由 QC 固定、归本人所有的输出，可以立即请求后继；它不等于公共执行，不产生公共赔付请求权，也不保证后继能获得签发容量。只有后继交易公开消费一个尚未创建的认证输出时，系统才原子地登记针对直接发行者的义务。到期并不等于立即赔付，赔付取决于公共提交和执行。成员拒绝是逐请求的本地决定：额度不足时不提交、不签名，幂等重试复用旧签名，不占用新额度。某个成员拒绝不构成全局闸门；没有 QC 也不等于没有局部占用。协议没有 Subject 级的 CAL 准入，也没有跨收集器的未完成上限，费用赞助政策也不是 CAL 准入。容量条件属于外部假设，不是已实现的机制。
