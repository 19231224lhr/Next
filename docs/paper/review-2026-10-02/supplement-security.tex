\section{Details of the Original-Execution Connection}
\label{supp:original-execution}

\noindent\textbf{Earliest conflicting polka.}
Fix a height and a commit certificate for nonempty value $v$ in round
$r$. Select two honest signers $K$ of that certificate. Each locks $v$
before issuing its precommit. The certificate can be assembled after
these signers have advanced; its observation time is irrelevant.

Suppose a higher-round polka for a different value exists, counting nil
if it permits unlocking. Select the smallest round $s>r$ with such a
polka and, within $s$, the earliest event $\tau$ at which its distinct
prevotes exist. Its three voters include a member $m\in K$. Because
honest rounds increase, $m$'s round-$s$ prevote follows its round-$r$
precommit. Without an unlocking justification, $m$ must prevote its
locked value. Relocking on $v$ in intervening rounds preserves this
restriction. A conflicting justification from a round below $s$
contradicts the choice of $s$; one from round $s$ must have existed before
$m$ cast the vote counted at $\tau$, contradicting the choice of $\tau$.
Evidence from above $s$ cannot precede a subsequent round-$s$ vote.
Thus no such polka exists. A higher-round conflicting commit would
require one. Same-round conflicting commits require an honest double
precommit. This argument depends on the stated lock-transition and
round-monotonicity rules, not timely receipt of commit evidence.

\noindent\textbf{Pairing, validation, and execution.}
Induct on candidate updates: local construction pairs a body with its
original parts; remote completion reconstructs the body from checked
parts. Moving Locked or Valid transfers both; switching targets clears
the old body. Historical installation does not mutate these active
candidates. A nonempty precommit follows a matching polka. An honest
prevote is justified by actual proposal validation or an earlier lock;
tracing the latter decreases the lock round and terminates at validation.
The final full-identity guard fixes the original transactions, height,
time, and other application context before Finalize. Synchronization
and replay supply the same original material. Hence same-height retries
cannot introduce a second original body through the stable-hash cache
within this call boundary.

\noindent\textbf{Meaning of the two equivalences.}
Batch refinement compares economic state after a whole batch with the
state after its specified sequence of single-item steps. It projects
away command identities, revision counters, and materialization tasks.
Replay equality instead fixes the entire original command sequence and
its block contexts; deterministic execution then reproduces each
application hash. These are different relations. Neither compares histories that order
parent arrivals and compensations differently. The delay-neutrality
theorem of the main text makes that comparison under its own hypotheses,
equating final CAL holders and reserve balances rather than application
hashes or revision metadata.
